Privacy Policy for TightnessPro
Last updated: May 2026
Overview
TightnessPro is a gas tightness testing calculator app developed by Themisto.app. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your information.
Data Collection
TightnessPro does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with any third party, server, or external service.
Data Stored Locally
The app stores the following information locally on your device only:

Engineer name
Gas Safe licence number

This information is stored using your device's local storage and never leaves your device. It is used solely to display on tightness test results within the app.
No Analytics or Tracking
TightnessPro contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or any third party tracking SDKs.
Children's Privacy
TightnessPro is intended for use by qualified gas engineers and is not directed at children under 13.
Changes to This Policy
Any updates to this privacy policy will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
